# Original-site inventory

Reviewed the public Squarespace site at [banxperformance.com](https://www.banxperformance.com/): Home, Services, Programs, Blog (including all three posts), and Contact.

- **Brand:** Squarespace palette defines black `#000000`, white `#FFFFFF`, green `#477732`, light green `#EDF6EA`, and deep green `#203617`. The stylesheet uses Georgia for headings and Times New Roman for body copy. The rebuild retains the green/black identity but uses a condensed sans-serif display face and a simpler sans-serif body.
- **Home:** BP mark and imagery, Banks Telkamp, CSCS, Denver-based coaching at Core Progression Elite Personal Training Sloan’s Lake, four values (belief, accountability, toughness, dedication), client testimonials, and @coachbanx on Instagram and Threads.
- **Services:** Consultation, Speed Key for youth and adults, custom online training, personal training, post-rehab return-to-sport training, and performance training. The original sends consultation and service leads to Trainerize.
- **Programs:** Greenhorn (beginner training), Bolt (speed and explosiveness), and Composite (strength and endurance), with individual Trainerize checkout links. The original lists $9, $10, and $10 USD per week respectively. The checkout pages are the source of current pricing.
- **Blog:** Elastic Performance, Enhancing Vertical Jump Performance, and Why Should Youth Athletes Lift Weights? All are credited to Johan Telkamp on the original.
- **Contact:** The original Squarespace form asks for name, email, and message. The rebuild defaults to the owner-specified inbox, `banxperformance@outlook.com`, for server-side delivery.
- **Media:** The original BP emblem, coach portrait, and blog artwork were sourced from the public Squarespace CDN and optimized locally. The homepage hero is a stock [weightlifting photo from Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/2261477/), not a photo of Coach Banx. Barlow Condensed and Manrope are hosted locally with their SIL Open Font licenses.
